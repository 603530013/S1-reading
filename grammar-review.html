👉 Unit 1 · Object Pronouns (受詞代名詞)

受詞代名詞 (object pronouns)：me, you, him, her, it, us, them
受詞代名詞通常放在動詞（或介系詞）後面
例：This is my mother. She loves me. ／ I am returning it to you.
例：Mr Lee is showing her a picture. ／ Do not disturb them.
例：We like stories. Our teacher is telling us a story.

🍅 Unit 3 · Singular and Plural Nouns (單數與複數名詞（規則與不規則）)

① 加 s：desk → desks、bag → bags
② 字尾 s, sh, ch, x 加 es：dress → dresses、box → boxes
③ 字尾 o 加 es：tomato → tomatoes、potato → potatoes
④ 子音 + y：去 y 加 ies：housefly → houseflies、puppy → puppies
⑤ f / fe 改成 ves：leaf → leaves、knife → knives
⑥ 不規則：child → children、man → men、woman → women、foot → feet、tooth → teeth、mouse → mice

👖 Unit 4 · Nouns (with No Singular Form) (沒有單數形的名詞)

大部分可數名詞的複數是加 s / es：tiger → tigers、brush → brushes、mango → mangoes

📏 Unit 7 · Comparison of Adjectives: Comparative Degree (形容詞比較級)

形容詞有三個等級：原級 (positive)、比較級 (comparative)、最高級 (superlative)
原級是形容詞的基本形：long、short、thick、clean、beautiful、interesting
比較兩個人、事、物時用比較級：longer、shorter、thicker、cleaner、more beautiful、more interesting
後面通常接 than：The black rope is longer than the white rope.
例：Mary’s dress is more beautiful than May’s dress. ／ The comic book is more interesting than the storybook.

🏆 Unit 8 · Comparison of Adjectives: Superlative Degree (形容詞最高級)

比較三個（或以上）的人、事、物時用最高級，前面通常加 the
tall → tallest、cheap → cheapest、long → longest、short → shortest、thick → thickest、clean → cleanest
長的形容詞用 most：most beautiful、most interesting
例：These ropes are long. The black one is the longest.
例：The flowers are beautiful. The rose is the most beautiful of all.

🅱️ Unit 9 · “Is”, “Are”, “Am”, “Was” and “Were” (be 動詞的現在式與過去式)

am, is, are 是現在式，was, were 是過去式
am 搭配 I；is 搭配 she, he 和單數名詞；are 搭配 you, they, we 和複數名詞
過去式：is / am → was；are → were
現在：She is seven years old. ／ They are in Primary 2A.
過去：She was six years old last year. ／ They were in Primary 1A last year.
時間提示：this year, now, every morning（現在）／ last year, yesterday, last week, last night（過去）

🤲 Unit 10 · “Has”, “Have” and “Had” (have 的現在式與過去式)

has, have 是現在式，had 是過去式
has 搭配 she, he 和單數名詞；have 搭配 I, you, they, we 和複數名詞
過去式：has / have → had（不分主詞）
現在：She has a pencil. ／ They have milk for breakfast every morning.
過去：I had measles last month. ／ They had eggs for breakfast yesterday.

🛠️ Unit 11 · “Do”, “Does” and “Did” (do 的現在式與過去式)

do, does 是現在式，did 是過去式
do 搭配 I, they, you, we 和複數名詞；does 搭配 he, she 和單數名詞
過去式：do / does → did
現在：You do the work here every day. ／ She does the work here every day.
過去：The boys did the work here yesterday.
回答問句：Do they …? → Yes, they do. / No, they don’t.；Does she …? → Yes, she does. / No, she doesn’t.；Did you …? → Yes, I did.

🕐 Unit 17 · Simple Present Tense (簡單現在式)
