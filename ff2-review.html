📐 文法：現在進行式 Present progressive

✍️ 加 ing 的拼法
規則例子
直接加 ingsail → sailing、play → playing、cook → cooking
短母音 + 一個子音：雙寫子音swim → swimming、run → running
字尾 e：去 e 再加 ingdive → diving、ride → riding、take → taking、write → writing

📐 文法：頻率副詞 always / sometimes / never

放在動詞前面
符號副詞例句
✓✓always（總是）We always go to the sports center.
我們總是去運動中心。
✓sometimes（有時）I sometimes go to the library.
我有時去圖書館。
✗never（從不）She never goes to the shopping mall.
她從不去購物中心。
⏱️ 時間介系詞 in / on / at
介系詞用在例句
in月份My birthday is in May.
on星期He plays soccer on Fridays.
at幾點鐘We get up at eight o'clock.

💪 Fluency Time! 3：Things we are good at

說法意思
very good at …很擅長…
quite good at …還算擅長…
very bad at …很不擅長…
terrible at …糟透了
